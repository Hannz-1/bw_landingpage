Your coach

Meet Joel Thomas

Joel Thomas is a psychology and sociology graduate with over 10 years of experience in coaching, specialising in body transformations. Before entering the fitness industry, Joel spent much of his time working within charities, supporting individuals from a wide range of backgrounds and demographics. These experiences shaped his understanding of people, behaviour and the importance of creating meaningful, lasting change.

Joel's passion for body transformations is also deeply personal. Having struggled with his weight for most of his life, he often referred to himself as the chubby brother who wore his T-shirt in the swimming pool. Having experienced those challenges first-hand, Joel understands how much weight can affect a person's confidence, identity and relationship with themselves. Today, his passion is educating people on how to eat well, feel good and get into the best shape of their lives.

Joel is also the founder of Base Wellness, delivering personal training services across London and developing the careers of other personal trainers through expert coaching education. He is incredibly passionate about every client's individual journey and cares deeply about creating genuine, long-term change.

His approach goes beyond simply achieving a physical transformation. He believes in educating people, building confidence and giving them the knowledge and skills to maintain their results long term.

Joel teaches you how to fish, rather than simply handing you the fish.

The programme

What is the Rebuild Project?

The Rebuild Project was designed to help individuals start again, rebuild their relationship with their bodies and become the person they want to be. Not just temporarily, but for the long term.

At its heart is the ABC Method. Most people jump straight from A to Z. They follow extreme diets, lose weight quickly, only to put it back on again. This creates a frustrating cycle of yo-yo dieting, inconsistent training and constantly starting over.

The Rebuild Project takes a different approach. I want to teach you how to understand your body, build sustainable habits and make decisions that support your health and performance for life.

One of the biggest frustrations I see is people believing that eating less is always the answer. In reality, eating more can often be part of the solution. We want to support your hormones, improve how you feel, build strength and help you perform at your best.

I am particularly passionate about teaching people how to lift confidently, safely and with longevity in mind. Strength training should be something you feel comfortable doing for the rest of your life.

I specialise in the 23 hours of the day outside the gym.

Getting yourself to the gym is fantastic, but what happens during the other 23 hours is just as important.

What are your daily habits? How are you managing stress? Are you recovering properly? Are your lifestyle choices allowing you to perform consistently?

Typically, the issue isn't simply what we eat or how many times we train. It's often rooted in how we feel about ourselves, our daily behaviours and the decisions we make regularly.

The Rebuild Project is about addressing those foundations, understanding your individual needs and creating a structure that works with your life.

This is about more than losing weight. It's about building a stronger relationship with food, developing confidence, improving your health and creating a body and lifestyle you can maintain.

The goal is simple: to help you rebuild, understand your body and never feel like you have to start again.
