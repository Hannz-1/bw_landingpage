Your free session

What to Expect

1

Step 1: Understand where you are now

We'll assess your current position, review your existing programme and identify the barriers that may be holding you back, whether that's time, knowledge, structure, motivation or consistency.

2

Step 2: Review your form and build your session

We'll review your PAR-Q before your session to help us understand your needs and prepare to deliver the best possible experience. We'll then build your session around you.

3

Step 3: Build your 30-day plan

We'll create a practical 30-day plan to move you in the right direction.

4

Step 4: No pressure

At the end of your session, we'll talk you through our services and explain how we can support you moving forward. There's no pressure to sign up. The session is about understanding your needs and helping you find the right approach.

Three Steps to Get Ready

1

Step 1: Eat, hydrate and prepare

  • Have something to eat before your session, but allow enough time for digestion.
  • Carbohydrates are preferable before exercise.
  • If you're short on time, choose an easily digested option such as fruit or cereal.
  • Stay hydrated and bring a bottle of water.
  • Feel free to have caffeine before your session if you wish.
2

Step 2: Send us your current programme

Please send us your current training programme and let us know what you've tried previously. This will help us understand your starting point and prepare for your session.

3

Step 3: Arrive early and warm up

Please arrive a little early and spend five minutes warming up on a rowing machine, treadmill or cross trainer before your session begins.
